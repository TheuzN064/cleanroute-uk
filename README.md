# CleanRoute UK

**CleanRoute UK** is a complete, modular, 100% offline-first React Native (Expo SDK 57, TypeScript, `expo-sqlite`) mobile app for residential and commercial cleaning service & route management in the United Kingdom.

## Features
- **100% Offline SQLite Architecture**: All clients, helpers, timers, and financial records reside locally on the device.
- **Resilient Wall-Clock Stopwatch**: Timer continues accurately even when phone screen is locked or turned off.
- **Dynamic Scheduling & Interactive Week Calendar**: 7-day strip with job count indicators, full month grid, helper assignment, and batch recurring scheduling.
- **Dedicated Payments Tab**: Real-time tracking of Receivables, Collected invoices, Staff Payouts, and Net Margins.
- **British English WhatsApp Invoices**: Instant UK bank transfer formatting (Sort Code, Account Number).
- **Turn-by-Turn Postcode Navigation**: Integrated Apple Maps routing for London & UK postcodes.
- **Bilingual (PT / EN) & Theme Support (Light / Dark Mode)**.
